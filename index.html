FAQ

Common
Questions

How long does it take to build my AI system? +
Most projects are completed within 3–14 business days depending on complexity. AI Chatbots take 3–5 days, Voice Agent Bundles take 5–7 days, Custom Tools take 7–10 days, and Full Business Automation takes 7–14 days. You receive a clear timeline and progress updates from day one.
Do I need any technical knowledge? +
None at all. We handle every technical aspect — from building and integrating to deploying and maintaining. You just describe what you want automated, and we take it from there. We also provide onboarding so you can manage things confidently on your end.
What if I'm not satisfied with the result? +
We offer a 100% client satisfaction guarantee with free revisions until you are completely happy. We do not consider a project complete until you sign off on it. A full refund is also available within 7 days of delivery if the service was not delivered as described in the agreed project scope.
How much can I save by automating my business? +
Most clients save 20–40 hours per week and reduce operational costs by 60–80% compared to hiring staff for the same tasks. The exact savings depend on your business size and which processes we automate — our free AI Audit will identify precisely where the biggest gains are for you.
Why is monthly maintenance required from Month 2? +
AI systems require continuous upkeep to remain accurate and effective. Knowledge bases go stale, platform APIs change, and conversation flows need ongoing tuning. The monthly maintenance plan covers all of this — ensuring your system stays sharp, your customers get accurate responses, and your business keeps running without interruption.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept Cash, Credit/Debit Card (Visa, Mastercard, Mada), Bank Transfer, STC Pay, PayPal, Apple Pay, and all major digital wallets. Payment details are provided after the free discovery call and proposal stage.
